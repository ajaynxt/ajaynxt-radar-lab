# AJAYNXT Radar Training Lab 🎯🛰️

Tactical Map Awareness • Drag Aim & Damage Simulator for Mobile & Desktop.

## Features
- **5 Selectable Avatars (Part 7):** Instant avatar switching with custom tactical SVGs and HUD integration.
- **Dual Display:** 
  - Tactical Minimap & Full-Map Grid (500m x 500m with A1-E5 sectors).
  - Simulated 3D Gameplay View with perspective floor and upper-chest drag reticle.
- **Drag Aim & Ballistics Range Lab (Part 5):**
  - Interactive target mannequin with realistic hitboxes (Head, Upper-Chest, Belly, Limbs).
  - Configurable fictional weapon profiles (Aero AR-4, Titan Marksman, Viper SMG).
  - Distance selector (15m, 35m, 65m) with simulated damage falloff and recoil bloom.
  - Real-time shot telemetry scoreboard & decal impact markers.
- **100% Offline & Independent:**
  - Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.
  - Zero external dependencies, zero trackers, ultra-low CPU footprint.
  - Completely independent of any real-game files or memory.
